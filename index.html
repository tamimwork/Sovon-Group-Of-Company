About Shovon

One group. Every step of the garment.

Shovon Group started in 1991 and grew into a vertically integrated manufacturer. Our own units handle knitting, dyeing, printing and accessories, so we control quality and lead time from yarn to carton.

We supply leading European and American brands with a workforce of more than 9,000 people.
